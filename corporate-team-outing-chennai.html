Questions HR asks us

Good to know.

What are good team outing places near Chennai?

Beach resorts along the ECR, Mahabalipuram for heritage, DakshinaChitra for culture, Pulicat Lake for a calm day out, and Pondicherry or Yelagiri for an overnight offsite. Our guide to team outing places near Chennai goes deeper.

Is a team outing the same as team building?

Not quite. A team outing is a day out together. It becomes team building when the day has a purpose and a programme. We design both, so your outing is relaxed and still brings people closer.

Half-day, full-day or overnight?

All three. A half-day suits a quick reset, a full day leaves room for games, lunch and a celebration, and an overnight offsite adds an evening together, which is often where the best conversations happen.

How many people can come?

From a small leadership team to a few hundred people. We plan the pace, the games and the groups around your numbers.

Can you work with a venue we've already booked?

Yes. We can plan the outing with you from the start, or bring the programme to a venue you already have in mind.

Ready for a day out together?

Tell us your team size, your dates and what you want to change. We'll design the day around it.
