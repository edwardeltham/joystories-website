CE+T Power: a Super Connect for a dream team

A full day that took a high-performing team from breaking the ice to breaking their own limits.

CE+T Power builds technology that keeps power flowing when it matters most. For their team, we designed Super Connect: a full-day journey that kept the energy flowing from the first hello to the final, unforgettable moment.

Break the ice, ignite the bond

Every great day starts with people feeling comfortable. The opening hour helped everyone relax, laugh and connect quickly, so that by the time the real challenges began, the room already felt like a team.

Thinking as one

Through the day, teams moved from physical coordination to strategy to an immersive challenge of deduction, where nobody held all the answers and communication was everything. Each stage asked a little more of the group: more listening, more trust, more willingness to let someone else lead.

Observe. Communicate. Solve.

Mind over matter

The day ended on a moment of personal courage, a reminder that the limits we feel are often the limits we believe. After a day of learning to rely on each other, people discovered they could also rely on themselves.

That is the full arc of our philosophy in one day: Us built through shared energy, We built through trust, and I built through the courage to step past old beliefs.

High-performing teams still need time to connect. Our outdoor and indoor team building days are built for exactly that. Let's talk.
