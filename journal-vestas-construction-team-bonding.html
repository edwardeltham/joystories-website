Vestas Construction: building teams like they build turbines

For the people who raise wind turbines, a half-day of team bonding rooted in the values that keep them safe and strong.

BUILD

The Vestas construction team does extraordinary work: raising wind turbines that power whole communities. On site, there is no room for guesswork. Every person depends on every other. We wanted their team bonding to feel the same way.

Energy first

We opened with fast, physical energisers that got everyone moving and laughing, perfect for a team that is more at home outdoors than in a meeting room.

Strategy and skill, together

Next, teams took on construction-inspired challenges that rewarded careful planning, precise communication and steady hands. The parallels with their real work were impossible to miss: a structure only stands when every part is placed with care, and when everyone knows their role.

Values you can feel

Woven through the afternoon were Vestas' own values: integrity, customer experience, teamwork, innovation and sustainable growth. Rather than reading them on a slide, teams brought each value to life in playful, personal ways, including literally walking in a colleague's shoes.

Wind means the world to them. So does the team beside them.

Why it lasts

Teams who build together in play carry that rhythm back to site: clearer calls, quicker trust, and a little more laughter on a long day. That is I to We to Us, at height.

Short on time? A half-day of team bonding can still shift how a team works together. Ask us how.
