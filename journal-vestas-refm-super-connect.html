Vestas REFM: one team across India and China

Team leaders from two countries and two working cultures, one day to become a single leadership team.

SUPER CONNECT

Vestas' Real Estate & Facilities Management team keeps the places where Vestas people work running smoothly. Its leaders sit across India and China: two countries, two working cultures, one shared responsibility. Super Connect was designed to turn a group of leaders who mostly meet on calls into one team that truly knows each other.

Break the ice, ignite the bond

Leaders are used to running the room. So we started by levelling it. Quick, warm, laughter-filled moments gave everyone permission to relax, and made it easy to connect across languages, accents and job titles within minutes.

Move, walk and think like a team

Next, the pace picked up. Fast challenges asked teams to communicate without assumptions, plan together and trust each other's calls. For leaders who usually hand out direction, it was a chance to experience what great followership feels like too.

Competition is good. Collaboration is great.

Across cultures, not around them

The heart of the day was collaboration across teams, where the best results came from sharing information openly and building on each other's ideas. Differences in how India and China approach problems stopped being friction and became an advantage: more perspectives, better solutions.

Observe. Communicate. Solve.

An immersive challenge of deduction put everything together. No single leader held the full picture. The only way through was clear communication, patience and trust in colleagues from the other side of the region.

Dare, dance, do it for glory

The day ended in celebration, with a room of leaders cheering each other on. That is our I to We to Us journey for a leadership team: confident individuals, a trusting group, and a shared identity that crosses borders.
