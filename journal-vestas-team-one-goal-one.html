Vestas: Team One, Goal One

A morning that took a busy project team from colleagues to collaborators, one chain reaction at a time.

ONE TEAM

Project teams live by deadlines, dependencies and handovers. For this Vestas team, we designed a morning that celebrated those same skills, and the people behind them, without a single spreadsheet in sight.

Getting to know the person behind the plan

We started where every strong team starts: with people. Light, playful moments helped colleagues discover each other outside of work, find unexpected things in common, and see each other as more than names on a project plan.

Move, think and win together

The middle of the morning brought high energy and a healthy dose of competition. Teams learned quickly that speed without coordination goes nowhere, and that the best results came when everyone's strengths were in play.

One chain reaction

We closed with a large, creative build that only worked if every small part worked, a perfect picture of a project. Brainstorming, problem solving and patience came together in one shared moment of triumph.

Let's start a chain reaction of happy teams.

From colleagues to collaborators

Breaking barriers, understanding each other's strengths, and plenty of laughter: the goals were simple. The result was a team that went back to work as one team, with one goal.
