Questions HR asks us

Good to know.

How do you celebrate Diwali at the office?

With a short, colourful moment at the work bay: rangoli, diyas, games and sweets, designed so everyone on the floor feels included. For a bigger evening, we run Diwali nights as corporate events.

Which festivals do you celebrate at work?

Pongal, Republic Day, Holi, Ramzan and Eid, Onam, Ganesh Chaturthi, Independence Day, Navratri, Diwali, Christmas and New Year, plus the fun days in between on our Joy Calendar.

Will it disrupt work?

No. Most celebrations are 7 to 12 minute moments at the desks. Work carries on, and the floor gets a lift.

Can you celebrate across several offices on the same day?

Yes. We plan and run festival celebrations across your offices, so every team gets the same moment.

Which festival is next on your floor?

Tell us your offices, your people and your dates. We'll handle the joy.
