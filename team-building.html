Three pillars of transformation

Every programme follows one journey.

I · Personal potential

Breaking the inner ceiling

Participants step into controlled discomfort, spot what holds them back and practise decisions under pressure.

Outcome: self-awareness, confidence, ownership.

We · Group potential

From my idea to our idea

Ego vs contribution, listening without defending, building on ideas instead of competing with them.

Outcome: collaboration, less friction, bigger solutions.

Us · Fun & energy

Fun is fuel, not a break

Breaking routine and fatigue, building emotional connection, creating shared moments of joy.

Outcome: renewed energy, stronger relationships.

Anatomy of a Joy Stories day

One day. Six acts.

A typical full-day programme, paced from the first hello to the last high five. Every act is tailored to your team.

01

Icebreakers

Break the ice, ignite the bond. Quick games that get everyone relaxed, talking and laughing in minutes.

Hello You · Hearty Handshake · Guns & Arrows · 2 to Tango

02

Team Engine

90 power-packed minutes that make you move, walk and think like a team. People who play together stay together.

Password · Blind Shapes · Team Tower · Team Tac Toe · Bomb Diffuse · Jumping Jack

03

Collaboration Games

Competition is good. Collaboration is great. Games that quietly reward the teams who choose to share.

Word Building · Cross Roads · Domino Effect

04

Rock & Roll

Chart paper, tape and one giant tank wheel. It's not just about the making, it's about making it to the finish line together.

05

Million Dollar Secret

Our signature mystery gala. Observe, communicate, solve.

06

We Dare You

Ten dares, done as a team. Pose like a photo, plank like plywood, dance for glory.

Strongest Man · Pose Like a King or Queen · Scissor Pose · Hook Steps
