Questions HR asks us

Good to know.

What corporate training programs do you offer?

Soft skills such as communication, leadership, emotional intelligence and conflict resolution; role-specific technical skills; and value instilling programmes, plus experiential programmes like Courage Code and The Gold Mine.

What is experiential training?

Learning by doing. Instead of lectures, people work through simulations and challenges that mirror real workplace situations, then reflect on what happened, so the learning carries back to the desk.

Do you run training in Chennai only?

We're based in Chennai and run most programmes here, at your office or a venue. Talk to us about other cities.
